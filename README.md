## 🌟 Theme Overview

**Threadline** is built natively on Shopify **Online Store 2.0**, combining editorial fashion aesthetics with modern conversion features:
- **Sections Everywhere:** Add, reorder, and customize blocks on any page template.
- **Zero Framework Bloat:** Ultra-fast loading built purely with lightweight custom elements, CSS custom properties, and native Liquid.
- **Conversion-Optimized Cart:** Dynamic free shipping threshold calculator, cart notes, and accelerated checkout buttons.
- **Shopify Feature Compliant:** Includes native Selling Plans / Subscriptions, Shop Pay Installments, Pickup Availability, and Complementary Product Recommendations.

## 🚀 Installation & Getting Started

### 1. Uploading the Theme
1. Log into your **Shopify Admin**.
2. Navigate to **Online Store > Themes**.
3. In the **Theme library** section, click **Add theme > Upload zip file**.
4. Select `Threadline-1.0.0.zip` and click **Upload**.

### 2. Previewing & Publishing
- Once uploaded, click **Actions > Preview** to inspect the theme with your catalog.
- When ready to launch, click **Publish** to make Threadline your live storefront.

## 🧭 Header & Navigation

Configure via **Theme Editor > Header**:
- **Sticky Header:** Toggle sticky navigation on scroll to keep search, account, and cart accessible.
- **Transparent Header:** Enable header transparency when overlaid on top of full-width hero banners.
- **Announcement Bar:** Display promotional messages, free shipping announcements, or multi-announcement tickers.
- **Predictive Search:** Real-time debounced autocomplete searching across products, collections, and journal articles.
- **Customer Accounts:** Integrated with `<shopify-account>` web component for seamless new customer account sign-in.
- **Country & Language Selectors:** Enable native market selectors for localized currencies and languages.

## 🎨 Hero & Editorial Sections

Threadline includes diverse storytelling sections to showcase your brand aesthetic:
- **Hero Banner:** Full-screen or split photographic banner with dual Call-to-Actions (e.g., *Shop Women* / *Shop Men*).
- **Editorial Split Section:** Large lifestyle imagery paired with curated brand editorial copy.
- **Featured Collection & Lookbook:** Clean product grids and carousel sliders with responsive columns (2–4 items).
- **Promotional Grid:** Asymmetrical fashion lookbook cards highlighting new arrivals or seasonal collections.
- **Editorial Journal (Blog & Articles):** Typography-first editorial reading experience with author profiles, tags, and comments.

## 🔍 Collection Discovery & Faceted Filters

- **Native Faceted Filtering (`collection.filters`):**
  - Instant filtering by price range slider, in-stock availability, color swatches, and size pills without reloading the page.
- **Active Filter Chips:** Visible active tag pills with one-click clear-all functionality.
- **Flexible Sorting:** Sort by Featured, Best Selling, Price Low/High, or Date Added.
- **Product Cards:** Secondary hover image reveal, discount sale tags, out-of-stock badges, and quick-add actions.

## 👗 Product Page Experience

Threadline’s Product Detail Page (PDP) is engineered for clarity and high conversion:
- **Responsive Media Gallery:** High-resolution product imagery with thumbnail navigation and zoom modal.
- **Variant Selectors:** Visual color swatches and button pills for apparel sizing.
- **Size & Fit Guide:** Interactive popup size guide with measurement tables.
- **Low-Stock Scarcity Badge:** Real-time stock alerts for low-inventory items to drive urgency.
- **Shop Pay Installments:** Automated installment payment schedules via `shopify-payment-terms`.
- **Pickup Availability:** Local store inventory lookup powered by `<pickup-availability>`.
- **Subscriptions / Selling Plans:** Native support for recurring purchase options.
- **Complementary Products:** Dynamic product recommendations to boost Average Order Value (AOV).
- **Sticky Add-to-Cart Bar:** Mobile-friendly sticky buy bar that stays accessible as customers read reviews.

## 🛒 Slide-out Cart Drawer

Threadline features an AJAX slide-out cart drawer that reduces checkout friction:
- **Free Shipping Calculator:** Dynamic progress bar showing how much more customers need to add to qualify for free shipping.
- **Order Notes:** Expandable textarea for gift messages and special delivery instructions.
- **Line-Level Discounts:** Clear display of applied discounts and savings.
- **Accelerated Checkout:** Direct one-click payment buttons for Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.
- **Empty Cart State:** Suggested collection links to keep shoppers exploring when the cart is empty.

## ⚙️ Theme Settings & Colors

Customize your entire storefront without writing code:
1. In the Shopify Theme Editor, click the **Theme Settings (Gear icon)** in the sidebar.
2. **Colors:** Adjust Primary Accent, Background, Text, Cards, and Button shades.
3. **Typography:** Select Google Fonts or Shopify native fonts for Headings and Body text.
4. **Layout:** Configure maximum page width (1200px to 1600px) and section vertical spacing.
5. **Product Badges:** Customize Sale, Sold Out, and New arrival badge labels and colors.
